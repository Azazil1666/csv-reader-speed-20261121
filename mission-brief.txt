THE SPEC (csv-reader 1.0.0's reading, exact):
- Text is read once, left to right, with commas and double quotes. A U+FEFF at its very start is dropped.
- A record ends at LF or CRLF; a line left with no characters is no record. Outside quotes a CR not followed by LF is dropped (error stray_cr).
- A field that opens with a quote runs to the next quote that is not doubled; "" inside it is one quote; each CRLF or lone CR inside it is read as LF; what follows its closing quote, up to the next comma or line break, is appended as it stands (quote_junk, when the field ends); a quote that never closes is a literal character, and the text after it is read again as unquoted (unterminated_quote).
- A quote inside an unquoted field is a literal character. Fields stay strings, never trimmed or converted.
- columns is the first record when header is true, else null; rows holds the records after it (every record when header is false), each padded with empty strings (short_row, with fields: its field count) or cut (long_row, with extra: the cut fields) to the first record's width.
- Each error is {code, line, row}, in the order met; a record's short_row or long_row when it ends: line is the 1-based line its record starts on, counting every LF, CRLF and lone CR inside quotes; row is its record's index in rows, null for the header.
- Answer is the JSON value {columns, rows, errors}.
